# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text is at that link and applies in full.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Standards

Expected: kindness and empathy, respect for differing opinions, constructive feedback given and received gracefully,
accountability for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks,
public or private harassment, publishing other people's private information without permission, and other conduct that
would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow them.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers via
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/sattva2020/keystone/security/advisories/new) (private) or through the
maintainers' GitHub profiles. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
